Pi 5 + UPS + Hailo: missing standoffs and GPIO height.

The UPS fits the Raspberry Pi 5 on its own. Add the Hailo AI HAT+ and the full stack also needs extra M2.5 standoffs plus a 2×20 extra-tall GPIO header. Both were required to create enough clearance and connect every layer correctly.

02 / KORA T-SHIRTS

Right paper. Right way round.

The complete route from Kora's locally generated file to a finished T-shirt. The critical difference: light-fabric paper is printed mirrored; dark-fabric paper is not.

START HERE

Prepare the artwork and shirt

  1. In Kora's panel choose one of four variants, correct the PL/EN text and approve it. Only then are the final print files and public voice QR code created.
  2. Make a test print on ordinary paper first. Check size, margins, text direction and whether both QR codes scan on a phone.
  3. Wash the shirt without fabric softener, dry it and iron it flat. Apply the transfer only to a clean, crease-free surface.
PRINTER

Set up the Epson ET-2861

  1. Use standard inkjet ink — not sublimation ink. PPD inkjet paper is not intended for laser printers.
  2. Feed one PPD sheet. In the driver select A4, high quality and matte/high-resolution paper, or the setting stated in the pack instructions.
  3. Set scaling to 100% and disable automatic fit if it changes the design size. Before pressing Print, check again: DARK = normal, LIGHT = mirrored.
  4. Do not touch wet ink. Lay the sheet flat and let it dry completely; PPD's dark-paper instructions recommend about five minutes.
DARK PPD

Black or coloured T-shirt

  1. Print print_ppd_dark_normal.png without mirroring. Trim close to the artwork — anything left around it remains as a white layer.
  2. Remove the paper backing before pressing. Place the thin transfer layer on the shirt with the printed artwork facing up.
  3. Cover with the supplied silicone paper. Iron: steam off, hard surface, firm even pressure and small circles; the official instructions give about three minutes for A4. Heat press: PPD gives 190°C, 12 seconds and high pressure as a guide.
  4. Wait about one minute, remove the silicone paper and inspect the edges. If any area has not adhered, cover it again and re-press.
LIGHT PPD

White or very light T-shirt

  1. Print print_ppd_light_mirrored.png — it is already mirrored. Trim excess paper around the design.
  2. Do not remove the backing before pressing. Place the printed side directly against the fabric, leaving the red PPD grid visible on top.
  3. Press on a hard, heat-resistant surface with steam off and firm, even pressure. Use the time and temperature printed in your specific PPD pack because batches and equipment can differ.
  4. Let the transfer cool and peel the backing as directed by PPD. Do not use this paper on a dark shirt — colours and whites will not reproduce correctly.
AFTER PRESSING

Set it, scan it, then wear it

  1. Before calling the shirt finished, scan both QRs on a phone away from home Wi-Fi. The voice code should open a public HTTPS page and play Kora's sentence.
  2. Wait at least 24 hours before the first wash. Wash the shirt inside out, avoid delicate/hand-wash cycles and do not tumble dry.
  3. Never place the iron directly on the finished print. If it needs refreshing, always protect it with silicone paper.
Most important:

The instruction sheet supplied with your exact PPD pack takes priority over this page. Use a hard, heat-resistant surface, switch steam off and never leave a hot iron or press unattended.
