NEW · WORKBENCH GOTCHA

Pi 5 + UPS + Hailo: missing standoffs and GPIO height.

The UPS fits the Raspberry Pi 5 on its own. Add the Hailo AI HAT+ and the full stack also needs extra M2.5 standoffs plus a 2×20 extra-tall GPIO header. Both were required to create enough clearance and connect every layer correctly.
